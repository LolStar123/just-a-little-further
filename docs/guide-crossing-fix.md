# Guide crossing repair

The route formerly reacquired the globally nearest point while airborne or landing. On self-intersections that could select another strand. Shortcuts also sampled beyond their final destination, so a jump could overshoot and reverse repeatedly.

- Retain arc-distance identity through jumps and commit the intended landing strand.
- Project grounded movement onto nearby route segments instead of snapping to sampled vertices.
- Limit shortcuts to the remaining route distance.
- Add target hysteresis so competing viewport perches do not alternate every frame.
- Keep attention hops local to the current strand.
- Rebase route distances between named scene anchors when layout or hill geometry changes.
- Keep physical position integrated; no actor position teleport is introduced.

Browser checks: London and Deadlock crossings settle near their target; the research loop reaches its perch during a longer stationary dwell. Desktop scrolling, mobile resize and a guide throw exercised. Pole-slide exits still kick left and right correctly. This covers the observed routes, not every possible user-deformed line.
